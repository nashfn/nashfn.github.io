About

I build systems that learn, then write down what I learned building them.

I'm currently a founder doing AI research in stealth. I'm working on Codewalk, a coding agent focused on understanding large codebases: how an agent can build a working mental model of a million-line repository and make changes that fit it.

Before that, I spent four years at Talkbox AI building on-device, privacy-first speech recognition, text-to-speech and agentic applications. Before that, I was at Google working on ML for traffic prediction, a system that scaled to 180 countries, two billion queries a day and millions of businesses, along with plenty of distributed-systems backend work.

This site is where I think out loud. I'll keep it to the four channels below.
